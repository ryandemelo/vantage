# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be civil. Assume good faith. Criticise the code rather than the person who wrote it.

## Expected behaviour

Give and receive feedback on the substance. Say what is wrong and what would be better. Accept that a maintainer may decline a change for reasons of scope or direction, and that this is not a judgement of you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, or publishing anyone private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that do not meet this standard, and may block accounts that repeat the behaviour.

Report a problem privately through GitHub Security Advisories or by contacting the repository owner directly. Reports are handled in confidence.
